# Custom-drone-with-flight-controller
This is my submission for hackclub blueprint. I created a custom drone with flight controller with a 3d printable frame.

I created a custom quadcopter accomponied with a Stm32 custom flight controller to use with any flight software. I will also be using a 4 in 1 ESC to make things simpler (SEQURE 65A 4 in 1). 

I have always wanted a drone, but never enough money or time to build one. Now that changes, I poured my heart into this project to be able to create the best drone I can make. I spent countless hours on pcb design and drone design research. Lets be real, who doesn't want a drone?? They are fun, and the hobby is always changing, meaning you swap parts out, and make your drone the best it can possibly be. I hope you feel the same way, and also follow my footsteps of becoming a quadcopter owner!!
